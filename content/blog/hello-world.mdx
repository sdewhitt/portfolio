Welcome to my blog!

## Why start a blog?
I wanted an excuse to write more and share the exciting things happening in my life.

Expect posts about:

- **Projects** — what/why I'm building
- **Music** — my passion outside of software development
- **Reflections** — thoughts on my career, hobbies, or just life in general

## What's this site built with?

This portfolio is built with [Next.js](https://nextjs.org),
[Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com), and TypeScript.
The blog itself is powered by **MDX** — Markdown with JSX support
— which means I can embed interactive components right inside posts.
